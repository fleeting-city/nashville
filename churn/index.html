Nashville food windows
open fast and close faster.

Every week Fleeting crawls Nashville for time-limited windows -- the dinners, pop-ups, residencies and runs that are here then gone -- and records when each one opens and closes. This is the churn cut of that record: how many food and drink windows opened versus closed each month, and how long the ones that closed actually lasted. One number, updated monthly, honest about how young it is.

In September 2026, Nashville saw 110 time-limited food and drink windows open and 115 close -- a net loss of 5 for the month. The food windows that closed that month had been open a median of 1 day.

How this is measured

The source is Fleeting's own weekly discovery crawl -- 17 snapshots between Jun 25 - Oct 22, 2026. A window is a distinct listing (its title and venue), counted once even if it appears in several weeks. It is a food window when its category, title or description matches Fleeting's shared food-and-drink vocabulary, or it comes from a known food source -- the same test our recommendation replies use. A window opens in the month its stated open date falls and closes in the month its stated close date falls; both must be real calendar dates. We only report a month once the crawl has watched it from its first day to its last -- a month we joined partway through, or the current unfinished month, is left out rather than reported short. Median days-open is counted inclusively from open to close (a one-night window is 1 day) over the food windows that closed in the record. Every number here is produced by build_churn_rate.py from the crawl record -- there are no hand-entered figures. The underlying table is published as JSON and CSV for anyone who wants to check or cite it. A short window that opens and closes between two weekly crawls can be missed: this is a sample of the curated time-limited windows Fleeting tracks, not a census of every restaurant change in Nashville.
