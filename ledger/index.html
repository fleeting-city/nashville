The Lost Nashville Ledger

A dated, citable record of Nashville food spots that have closed for good. 3 closures on record and counting -- never a guess, never a pop-up that merely ended, only places confirmed gone with a real close date. The record runs from August 2026 to September 2026.

How to cite this ledger

This is a public, citable reference. To credit it in a story, post or dataset, link the canonical page and name the ledger:

Fleeting Lost Nashville Ledger, https://nashville.fleeting.city/ledger/

Every entry has a stable permalink -- click cite this entry on any row to link that exact closure. The underlying record is published as open data (JSON, CSV) under CC BY 4.0 -- free to reuse with attribution.

We keep the record nobody with ads to sell can keep. Fleeting is a free weekly on what is only here for a moment in Nashville -- food, drink, pop-ups and events -- so you catch the next one before it joins this list.
