How Fleeting started

We kept hearing
about it too late.

We got tired of finding out about Nashville pop-ups after they were already over. We'd see the photos a day later and wonder how everyone else knew.

We tried to solve it by following ten newsletters, twenty Instagram accounts, venue calendars, and group chats. It worked, sort of. Keeping up started to feel like a job.

So we made Fleeting: one Thursday email with the Nashville pop-ups we don't want to miss, and don't want you to miss either.
