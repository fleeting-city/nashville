How this is ranked

The source is Fleeting's own weekly discovery crawl of time-limited Nashville windows. A window earns SOLD OUT only from a real availability check or unambiguous "sold out" language in its own listing - never a guess. FILLED FAST means the listing carried genuine scarcity language ("going fast", "few tickets left") and the window then closed this past week. Everything is ranked by how brief and in-demand the window was, using the same fill and close-speed computation as our members' retrospective. Dates are the listing's own; a missing date is left off, not invented. A window that simply reached its end date with no sold-out or scarcity signal is not on this board - that is a different list.
