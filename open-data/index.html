How this is built

The source is Fleeting's own weekly discovery crawl -- the whole board for the newest run (bundle 2026-10-22), not a hand-picked subset. A row is published only when the listing is one we could verify, carries a real, parseable closing date, and has not already closed; a window with a vague "end of summer" and no datable close is left out rather than guessed. closes is the last day to catch a run or the date of a one-off; days_left is counted from the build date. Of 155 board items this run, 93 met that bar (1 undated, 55 unverified, 6 already closed were dropped). Every field is the source listing's own; nothing is invented. The machine-readable as_of timestamp in the JSON says exactly how fresh the feed is. This is a sample of the curated time-limited windows Fleeting tracks, not a census of everything in Nashville.

Most of these close within days. That is the whole problem Fleeting exists for. The Thursday list is the week's time-limited Nashville windows in your inbox while they are still open. No alerts, no notifications; one email a week.
