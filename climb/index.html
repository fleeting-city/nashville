Why this only works because we are small

A counter climbing to 100 is only interesting while the numbers are small enough that one person changes them. Once a list is the one everybody in Nashville already has, "watch us grow" stops meaning anything -- the number moves whether you showed up or not. So this is a window that closes on itself: there is exactly one climb from nothing to 100, and you are standing in it. That is not a growth hack bolted on; it is the same thing Fleeting is about -- the stuff that is here, then gone.
