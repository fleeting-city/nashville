      How this is built

      Every spot here is a time-limited Nashville window the Fleeting crawl actually tracks, with a real, parseable close date -- the same honest definition /closed/, /w/ and /settle/ use. A spot you can watch maps to a tracked source; a spot with only a vague "end of summer" and no datable close is left off rather than guessed. A spot stays on the board while it is open and for 30 days after it closes, so a watch for a just-closed spot still reads honestly; after that it drops off and your watch shows it as no longer tracked -- we never pretend to watch something we cannot see. Your watchlist lives in your own browser and in this page's link; we store nothing about you, and we never send you anything. The machine-readable feed is watchlist.json.
