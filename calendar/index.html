Subscribe once. Every current Nashville here-then-gone window - the pop-up with a last day, the one-night show, the seasonal menu about to end - shows up on your own Google or Apple calendar with the day it closes. As the crawl finds new ones, they appear on their own. You never have to check back.

Add this calendar →

Tapping that opens your calendar app and offers to subscribe. If it does not, copy the address below and add it by hand:

  • Google Calendar: Other calendars → From URL → paste the address.
  • Apple Calendar: File → New Calendar Subscription → paste the address (or just tap Add this calendar on iPhone).
  • Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web → paste the address.

This calendar does not notify you. It is a pull surface on purpose - no alerts, no reminders, nothing buzzing in your pocket. Each window is just a quiet marker sitting on the day it closes. You look when you look.
